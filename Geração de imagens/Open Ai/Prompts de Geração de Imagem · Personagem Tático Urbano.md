## Prompt Engineering Doc · Personagem Tático Urbano Masculino

> [!abstract] ANÁLISE DO PERSONAGEM-FONTE
> Personagem masculino em estilo anime/manhwa semi-realista. Arte de linha limpa com shading plano e gradientes suaves estética próxima de ilustração de light novel japonesa + manhwa coreano de ação contemporânea.
> **Referências de estilo:** GGO aesthetic (SAO II), manhwa de ação urbana, streetwear tático.

## 🤖 GPT IMAGE 2.0 PROMPT COMPLETO

> [!example] GPT IMAGE 2.0 · PROMPT PRINCIPAL
> **Versão:** ChatGPT ImageGen 2.0
> **Aspect Ratio:** 2:3 (portrait)
> **Resolução recomendada:** 1024×1536

```
Create a high-quality semi-realistic anime illustration of a young adult male character standing in a confident, relaxed pose against a clean, light neutral background (off-white or very pale gray). This is a full-body character design sheet in the style of a Japanese light novel or Korean manhwa with crisp, clean line art and carefully controlled flat color shading with subtle soft gradients for volume.

CHARACTER PHYSICAL DESCRIPTION:

The character is a young man, approximately 17 to 22 years old, with a lean and moderately tall build. His overall vibe is that of a calm, self-assured urban operative or a skilled player in a tactical game environment.

HAIR:
Medium-length dark brown hair, naturally straight with a slight messiness. It falls loosely around the face, partially covering the forehead and framing the cheeks. The hair reaches near the collar of the jacket. Strands are slightly disheveled in a deliberate, cool way not sloppy, but effortlessly stylish.

FACE AND EXPRESSION:
Semi-closed eyes with a calm, slightly smug and self-confident expression the kind of look that says he knows exactly what he's doing. The gaze is slightly downward and sideways, as if looking at something beneath his notice. His features are clean and angular, with a defined jaw and high cheekbones. He wears thin rectangular glasses with a dark frame small, precise, slightly tilted.

HEADWEAR:
A black baseball cap worn backwards the brim faces behind. The cap has a thin bright orange-amber stripe detail along the brim's edge. The interior of the cap's brim is white or off-white, slightly visible from the front. The cap sits snug but relaxed on his head, partially pressing down on his hair.

OUTER LAYER TACTICAL JACKET:
An oversized tactical military-style long coat reaching approximately mid-thigh. The primary color is army green / olive drab (a muted, desaturated military green). The jacket features bold bright orange accent panels or stripes along the sleeves and collar area. The interior lining of the jacket is crisp white, visible because the jacket hangs open in the front. The jacket has a heavy tactical/outdoor feel: visible pockets with flap closures, D-ring clips and buckle straps, heavy-duty zipper pulls, and textured fabric panels suggesting weather resistance. The collar is a high tactical-style collar that frames the neck. On the jacket chest area, there 
is a small circular patch resembling a "no entry" or prohibition symbol in red and black. 
Additional small gear clips or tag accessories hang from the jacket's interior straps. 
The jacket sleeves are slightly pushed or rolled back, revealing the white inner lining.

INNER LAYER SHIRT:
Underneath the jacket, the character wears a fitted black t-shirt. Centered on the 
chest, the text REMEMBER is printed in bold distressed red lettering the font 
should look slightly horror-adjacent or gothic, with rough edges as if worn or 
spray-painted. The text is clearly visible through the open jacket front.

LOWER BODY:
The character wears standard blue denim jeans medium wash, straight or slightly 
tapered fit, with natural wear creases. Simple and grounded, balancing the tactical 
complexity of the jacket.

WEAPONS AND HOLSTERS:
Inside the jacket, along both interior sides, there are tactical holsters. Two pistols 
are partially visible one on each side suggesting the character is armed but not 
overtly aggressive. The guns are sleek, dark-colored semi-automatic pistols. Their 
grips and slides are just barely visible beneath the open jacket panels, adding 
menace without being the focus of the composition.

POSE AND COMPOSITION:
The character stands in a relaxed, confident three-quarter stance. Arms hang naturally 
at his sides, slightly away from the body. His head is tilted very slightly, adding 
a casual edge to an otherwise controlled posture. The pose communicates "I'm dangerous 
but I don't need to prove it." The figure takes up the central 60% of the vertical 
frame, with generous breathing room on all sides.

ART STYLE SPECIFICATIONS:
Render this in a clean, modern anime/manhwa semi-realistic illustration style. 
The line art should be sharp, consistent in weight, with slightly thicker outlines 
on the outer silhouette and thinner interior lines. Color fills should be flat but 
with controlled soft cel-shading 2 to 3 tone values per area, subtle rim lighting 
from above. No heavy painterly textures this is a clean character illustration, 
not a painting. The overall color palette is dominated by: dark brown (hair), 
black (cap, shirt, glasses, gun handles), army olive green (jacket), bright orange 
(accent details), white (jacket lining), and medium blue (jeans). Background: 
clean solid off-white or very light gray no gradients, no environment, pure 
character focus.

QUALITY DIRECTIVES:
- Perfect facial anatomy and proportions consistent with anime semi-realism
- Clean, confident line art with no sketch artifacts
- Accurate clothing folds and fabric physics especially on the oversized tactical jacket
- Holsters and tactical gear rendered with functional logic
- Text on shirt must be clearly legible: EXACT TEXT: 'REMEMBER' in red distressed font
- Avoid overrendering keep the clean, graphic quality of a professional character sheet
- No background elements, no environmental lighting, no secondary characters
- The character should feel like a finished, publishable illustration for a light novel or manhwa
```

> [!tip] DICAS DE REFINAMENTO GPT Image 2.0
>
> - Se o texto "REMEMBER" sair ilegível → adicionar na regeneração: `Make the text REMEMBER on the shirt larger, more centered, clearly readable in red`
> - Se a jaqueta perder os detalhes laranja → reforçar: `The orange accent panels on the jacket sleeves must be vivid bright orange, not muted`
> - Se o fundo ganhar elementos → adicionar ao final: `Absolutely no background elements. Pure white/gray background only.`
> - Se os óculos sumirem → adicionar: `He must wear thin rectangular dark-framed glasses on his face`
> - Para variação com fundo urbano → substituir o bloco de background por: `Urban alleyway at night, rain-slicked pavement, distant neon reflections in the wet ground, atmospheric fog the character stands in foreground, sharply lit`

> [!warning] PARÂMETROS NIJI 7 NOTAS TÉCNICAS
>
> - `--s 180` é o ponto ideal para manter o estilo anime sem oversaturation. Subir para `--s 250` se quiser resultado mais estilizado e dramático.
> - Para mais fidelidade ao design original (menos interpretação artística), use `--s 100`
> - Se os óculos sumirem: adicionar `glasses on face` com peso reforçado no início do prompt

> - Se a jaqueta perder os coldres internos: adicionar `interior holsters with pistol grips visible`
